Recharge guide
Recharge pit
Easy · DIY

Dig a 3×3 ft pit, 4 ft deep near your borewell. Fill with gravel, sand, and charcoal layers. Rainwater filters down and recharges the aquifer below.

Rooftop rainwater harvesting
Moderate

Connect roof drainpipes via a first-flush filter to a storage tank. Overflow from the tank can be directed into a soakpit to recharge the groundwater.

Borewell recharge shaft
Advanced

Sink a 6 inch PVC pipe 20–30 ft into the ground adjacent to the borewell. Add a sand-gravel filter at the top. During rains, water flows directly into the aquifer.
